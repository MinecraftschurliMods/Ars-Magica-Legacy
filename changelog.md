# Additions

- New biome: Witchwood Forest
    - A mix of Dark Forest, Flower Forest and normal Forest
    - Has a purple hue on grass and most leaves
    - Witchwood Trees spawn here frequently, as well as Dark Oak, Oak and Birch trees
    - All flowers normally spawning in Flower Forests spawn here, as well as all five types of flowers added by the mod
    - All normal animals and monsters spawn here, as well as rabbits, bees and wolves
    - Dryads and Mana Creepers spawn twice as commonly here
    - Liquid Etherium lakes can spawn here, in addition to their normal Plains spawning
    - Moonstone meteorites and amethyst geodes spawn more commonly here
